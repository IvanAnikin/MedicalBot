Currently on metformin 500 milligrams twice daily, lisinopril 10 milligrams daily, and aspirin 81 milligrams daily. Allergic to sulfa drugs, causes rash. Family history significant for father who had myocardial infarction at age 58. Patient is a former smoker, quit 5 years ago, 20 pack-year history. On examination, patient appears diaphoretic and anxious. Blood pressure 158 over 92, heart rate 96 beats per minute, respiratory rate 22, oxygen saturation 94 percent on room air, temperature 98.6 degrees Fahrenheit. EKG shows ST depression in leads V4 through V6 and T-wave inversions in leads I and aVL. Initial troponin elevated at 0.15 nanograms per milliliter. Assessment: Unstable angina, need to rule out non-ST elevation myocardial infarction.
